# CareConnect AI

## Project Overview
CareConnect AI is an AI-powered healthcare appointment assistant built using IBM watsonx Orchestrate.

The project is designed to help busy working mothers, elderly individuals, and people who struggle with technology find doctors who accept their insurance and have the earliest available appointments.

## How It Works
1. The patient enters the type of doctor they need.
2. The AI asks for their ZIP code and health insurance plan.
3. The patient provides their preferred appointment date.
4. The AI uses sample doctor information to identify matching doctors.
5. The AI compares sample appointment availability.
6. The patient selects an appointment.
7. The AI simulates the booking and provides confirmation.

## Technology
- IBM watsonx Orchestrate
- AI agent instructions and conversational workflows
- Sample doctor database

## Prototype Demonstration
The prototype was tested with a patient looking for a dermatologist in Brooklyn, ZIP code 11229, with Aetna PPO insurance.

The AI identified matching fictional doctors, compared sample appointment dates, and simulated an appointment booking.

## Limitations
This is an educational demonstration. All doctor information and appointment times are fictional. The prototype does not connect to real healthcare scheduling systems or book actual appointments.

## Project Goal
Make healthcare appointment scheduling simpler, faster, and more accessible for everyone.
